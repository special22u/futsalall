The events that define the international game

International futsal runs on a clear cycle of major tournaments. These are the five events that matter most — the ones that move the rankings, make reputations and produce the sport's best matches.

FIFA Futsal World Cup

The biggest prize in the sport, held every four years with the best national teams from every confederation. Brazil and Spain have historically dominated it, and winning it defines a generation of players. Qualifying runs through the continental championships, so the road to the World Cup shapes the whole calendar.

UEFA Futsal EURO

Europe's championship and, given the strength of its professional leagues, arguably the highest week-to-week standard in international futsal. Spain, Portugal, Russia and Italy have all lifted the trophy, and the finals tournament is where tactical trends in the modern game usually appear first.

Women's Futsal EURO

The showcase of the fast-growing women's game. Spain set the early standard by winning the first editions, and each tournament has been more competitive than the last as more federations invest. With a Women's Futsal World Cup now part of the landscape too, this is the side of the sport growing quickest.

Futsal Africa Cup of Nations

Africa's championship, doubling as the route to the World Cup for the continent's teams. Morocco has become the dominant force with a fully professional setup at home, while Egypt and others keep the competition honest — the atmosphere at AFCON finals is among the loudest in world futsal.

Futsal Finalissima

A newer intercontinental showpiece bringing together the champions and leading teams of Europe and South America — the two historic heartlands of the sport — in a short, high-quality finals event. It is essentially a mini world championship, and an easy tournament for new fans to start with.
