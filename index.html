Futsal is football at full speed: five players a side, a smaller low-bounce ball, and a hard court where every touch counts. It is the game that shaped generations of world-class footballers, and it has a thriving international scene of its own — World Cups, continental championships and professional leagues watched by millions. Yet good, clear English-language coverage of it has always been hard to find.

That is why FutsalAll exists. It is an independent futsal publication covering the sport properly: daily tournament news, the FIFA rankings explained and updated, and plain-language guides to how the game actually works. No filler, no recycled press releases — just useful reading for fans, new players and coaches alike.
